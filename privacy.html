Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

ShortApp is an internal tool operated by Ton Nom. It is used only with accounts we own; it does not collect data from the public or from other users.

1. Data we access

  • Google / YouTube (YouTube API Services): an authorization token for our own channel, with the youtube.upload scope, used only to upload our clips; and public video statistics (titles, view counts, durations) of partner creators, used to choose which videos to clip.
  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): an authorization token for our own TikTok account (user.info.basic, video.upload), used only to send our clips to that account.

2. How data is stored and used

Authorization tokens are stored only on the computer running ShortApp, in local files. They are never sold, shared or sent to any other party, and are used only to upload videos to our own accounts. Public statistics are used at the time of analysis and are not stored beyond 30 days.

3. Retention and deletion

We delete tokens and cached data when they are no longer needed, and on request. You can also revoke ShortApp's access at any time: for Google, at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; for TikTok, in the app under Settings > Security > Apps and services.

4. Third parties

ShortApp uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy. It also uses TikTok for Developers; see the TikTok Privacy Policy. Text analysis may be performed by a third-party AI provider on transcripts of published creators' videos; no personal data of other users is sent.

5. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: ton@email.com.
