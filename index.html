The best moments of partner creators, in vertical format.

ShortApp is an internal desktop tool that turns long videos from creators we work with into short, subtitled vertical clips, and publishes them on our own YouTube and TikTok accounts, always crediting the original creator.

Creators' consent

We only clip creators who gave us written permission. Every clip credits them and links to the original video.

Edited, not reuploaded

Clips are trimmed, reframed for 9:16, subtitled and labelled by speaker.

Our accounts only

The tool posts only to accounts owned by Ton vrai nom. It does not act on behalf of other users.

How it works

1. A partner creator's video or clip is selected. 2. It is transcribed and the strongest moment is cut. 3. The clip is reframed, subtitled and credited. 4. It is uploaded to our YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and to our TikTok account as a draft through the TikTok Content Posting API, where a team member reviews and publishes it.
